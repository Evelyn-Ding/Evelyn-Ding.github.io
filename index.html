About Me

New York City, NY

Evelyn Ding

Hello! My name is Evelyn Ding. I'm studying Computer Science at Columbia University and I'm interested in its applications to speech and natural language processing.
My research interests lie in the intersection of AI and healthcare, and my current research at the Speech Lab focuses on the modality of audio in identifying vocal biomarkers to develop interpretable AI models for disease detection.
My interests include reading, photography, and documenting places I've been. I feel lucky to go to school in NYC — the city is full of vibrant energy, and I find inspiration everywhere I look.
